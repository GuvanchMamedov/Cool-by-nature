COOL BY NATURE – INSTALLABLE APP (Team Heatbreakers)
====================================================

This folder is an installable web app (PWA). To install it, it must be
opened from an https:// link. Double-clicking index.html still works,
but then it can't be installed.

OPTION A – GitHub Pages (free, permanent link)
1. Sign in at github.com and create a new PUBLIC repository,
   e.g. "cool-by-nature".
2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in EVERYTHING inside this
   folder (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the icons folder).
   Click "Commit changes".
3. Go to Settings > Pages > "Deploy from a branch" > main / (root) > Save.
4. After 1–2 minutes your link is:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/cool-by-nature/

OPTION B – Netlify Drop (fastest)
1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign in (free), so the site stays up.
2. Drag this whole folder onto the page. You get an https:// link at once.

INSTALLING THE APP
- Laptop (Chrome or Edge): open the link and click the Install icon at the
  right end of the address bar.
- Android (Chrome): menu (three dots) > "Install app" / "Add to Home screen".
- iPhone/iPad (Safari): Share > "Add to Home Screen".
After the first visit it works fully offline.

UPDATING LATER
Replace index.html, and change VERSION in sw.js (e.g. 'cbn-v2') so
installed copies pick up the new version.
